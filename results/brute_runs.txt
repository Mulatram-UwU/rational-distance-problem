### NOTE ON TIMINGS ###
The runs above were taken while another process (fast_search3.exe, owned by a
different workload in this same repo) was keeping all 12 cores busy, which
inflates wall times by roughly 2.4x.  Repeated measurement matrix taken under
that same load (3 consecutive runs each, seconds):
  N=500   0.339  0.274  0.282
  N=1000  0.605  0.623  0.522
  N=2000  3.123  2.997  3.119
  N=3000  9.747  9.463  9.280
When the competing work paused, the same runs took 0.023 / 0.155 / 1.262 / 3.928 s
(N=5000: 22.699 s, N=10000: 235.905 s).  Candidate counts and solution counts are
load-independent.  All candidate counts equal sum_{Q=1..N} (Q-1)^2 (interior) and
sum_{Q=1..N} (Q+1)^2 (--symmetric), e.g. N=3000: 8,995,500,500 and 9,013,506,500.
